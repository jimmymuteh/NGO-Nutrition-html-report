Nutrition Program Update — 2026-11-15

Nairobi NGO Six-Month Outcome Briefing | Printable & Shareable Summary

Across 180 participants tracked over six months in Kitui, Machakos, Nairobi, kitui delivered the strongest weight recovery across the program, families who showed up regularly saw steadier weight gains, and younger children bounced back faster than older participants—giving the program team a clear picture of where support is working best and where follow-up sessions can lift recovery further.
